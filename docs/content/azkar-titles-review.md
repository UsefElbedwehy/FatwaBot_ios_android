# عناوين الأذكار — للمراجعة قبل النشر

Proposed titles for the azkar corpus, so each card has an identity instead of a
source line, and so search matches a name rather than the matn.

**Nothing here is applied.** These are religious content: a title is an
interpretation of what a dhikr *is*, and a wrong one mislabels a supplication on
a screen people read daily. They go into a migration only after review — the same
gate the hadith corpus went through.

## Where these come from

Taken from the standard حصن المسلم chapter headings and the common naming for
each dhikr (سيد الاستغفار, آية الكرسي, التهليل …), not invented. Where a text has
no conventional name, the title describes what it asks for rather than inventing
a label.

## How to review

Change the "العنوان المقترح" column directly in this file, or mark a row
`؟` and I'll bring alternatives. The `#` column is the entry's `sort_order`,
which is how the migration will key onto rows.

## Three I am least sure of

1. **`افتتاح الأذكار` (row 0, both)** — this is a preface, not a dhikr with a
   traditional name. It may read better untitled, which the reader already
   supports.
2. **`شهادة التوحيد` (morning 8 / evening 9)** — حصن المسلم gives this no short
   name; the title describes its content.
3. **`الاستعاذة من شر النفس والشيطان` (morning 13 / evening 14)** — long. A
   shorter `اللهم عالم الغيب والشهادة` would match how it is usually referred to.

# Two content problems found while titling

These are not title questions. Titling forced a read of all 94 entries, and two
things surfaced that are worth fixing in the corpus itself.

## 1. A duplicated entry

**الكرب والهم rows 1 and 7 are the same dhikr**, character for character. The
category shows the same card twice. Titling cannot hide that — it makes it more
obvious, because both rows would carry the same name.

Recommend deleting row 7 and letting the category be 7 entries.

## 2. Several entries are instructions, not dhikr text

A number of rows lead with the narrator's framing rather than the words to say:

- الطعام 0 — «إذا أكل أحدكم طعامًا فليقل: بسم الله»
- الطعام 5 — «إذا دعي أحدكم فليجب…»
- السفر 2 — «قال جابر: كنا إذا صعدنا كبرنا»
- النوم 0 — «يجمع كفيه ثم ينفث فيهما فيقرأ فيهما:»
- النوم 11 — «يقرأ ﴿الم﴾ تنزيل السجدة، وتبارك»

A reader tapping نسخ on الطعام 0 copies "إذا أكل أحدكم طعامًا فليقل بسم الله"
rather than "بسم الله". The instruction belongs in `virtue_note`, with the dhikr
itself in `arabic_text`.

Worth deciding before titles land, because the split changes what the title
should describe.
